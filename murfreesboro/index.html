How Murfreesboro Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

A little routine care makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so small issues get caught early.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and also shows rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement in peak season.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
