Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Johnson City HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service built around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and leaves staff miserable during the rush. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

Replacement and maintenance plans are planned alongside owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we install during off-hours, keeping your kitchen cooking and the dining room comfortable throughout.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.
